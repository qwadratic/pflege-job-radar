## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Found 2026-10-01 by TASK-184 (LLM title-vs-body review of all 2,794 open postings with a description; every example below read by hand). 404 postings (14.5%) are filed under a registry site whose town/operator the text contradicts. Mechanism (read from code): app/crawl.py stamps loc=[seed town] + city_source='seed' when a shared board has no structured location and the body has no 'am Standort X'; pflege_jobs/cli.py::_city_inherited makes the Matcher treat that city as unknown (ck=None); Matcher._match_board's single-clinic-pool branch then returns R0_board 0.9 for ANY posting (TASK-59A/TASK-66 only refuse a KNOWN disagreeing city). Own-board postings are right to pass; group-wide boards (AMEOS portal lists the whole group, Swiss titles included) and aggregators are not. Counts of judged site mismatches by board: krankenpflegejobs24.de 173/181 (all under clinic 66103 Frauenklinik Aschaffenburg, 30 beds, whose careers_url IS the aggregator city page https://www.krankenpflegejobs24.de/klinik-am-ziegelberg-frauenklinik-aschaffenburg; employers elsewhere in Hessen, e.g. Bad Orb, Frankfurt, Erlensee), karriere.ameos.eu 50/57 under AMEOS Neuburg (Kiel, Stassfurt ...), klinikum-straubing.de 17/35, awo-omf.de 13/13, karriere.rottalinnkliniken.de 12/13 (Eggenfelden/Pfarrkirchen jobs under Simbach), karriere.barmherzige.net 12/20 (Altenheim jobs under Krankenhaus BB Muenchen), kliniken-gz-kru.de 12/36, jobs.diakoneo.de 11/101, karriere.klinikum-nuernberg.de 11/49, dongku.de 9/13, karriere.klinikverbund-allgaeu.de 9/33 (Oberstdorf/Immenstadt/Mindelheim under Kempten), recruitingapp-5511 (ANregiomed Rothenburg under Ansbach), bewerbung.arberlandkliniken.de (Zwiesel under Viechtach). Effect on the 10,000-bed cohorts: raw 22.5..30.7 vs 20.8..27.7 vacancies per 1000 beds without the mismatches; the smallest hospital cohort showed 64 before the aggregator was split off.
